What goes into a backup. Each ZIP includes a full export of your app data (settings, projects, recordings metadata, transcript segments), your project files on disk (transcripts, codes, annotations), optional packaged ODT exports, and a manifest. Turn on Include recordings (audio, video) to also copy the original media files; leave it off to keep archives smaller while still preserving text and structure.

Your data. Your interviews and research material are of extreme importance to the developer of this project. Backups are designed to help you stay in control of your work—use them together with any other safeguards you rely on.
